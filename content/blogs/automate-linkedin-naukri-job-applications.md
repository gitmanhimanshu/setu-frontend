Finding the perfect job online is tedious. You browse LinkedIn, find a role, copy the requirements, tailor your resume, track down the recruiter's email, and finally write a cold email. 

With **Setu**, you can automate this entire workflow. By combining web-scraping plugins (like Firecrawl) with LLMs (Claude or ChatGPT) and Setu's Gmail MCP integration, your AI assistant does all the heavy lifting.

## Step 1: Connect Your Plugins
In your AI client (like Claude Desktop or Cursor), connect the following plugins:
- **Setu MCP**: For drafting and sending emails via your Gmail account.
- **Firecrawl (or similar)**: For extracting web page content from LinkedIn and Naukri URLs.

## Step 2: The Magic Prompt
Instead of manually doing the research, simply paste the job URL into your AI chat and use a prompt like this:

> "Here is a job posting from Naukri: [URL]. Please extract the requirements, tailor my resume (attached) to highlight relevant skills, find the recruiter's email if available on the page, and use Setu to draft an application email."

## Step 3: AI Does the Work
The AI will:
1. Scrape the provided LinkedIn or Naukri URL.
2. Analyze the job description.
3. Cross-reference it with your PDF resume.
4. Draft a highly personalized, compelling email.
5. Use Setu to securely queue the email as a draft in your Gmail.

## Step 4: Review and Send
Nothing leaves your inbox without your approval. You can review the drafted email on the Setu Dashboard, check the open tracking signals to see if the HR manager read it, and automate your follow-ups if they haven't responded within 3 days.

Stop wasting hours on manual data entry. Let AI handle the application, so you can focus on the interview.
