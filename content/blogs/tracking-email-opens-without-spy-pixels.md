## The Problem with Traditional Email Tracking

For years, marketers and sales professionals have relied on invisible 1x1 tracking pixels to know if an email was opened. When the recipient's email client loads the image, the server records an "open".

However, this method is fundamentally flawed today.

### Apple Mail Privacy Protection (MPP)
In 2021, Apple introduced Mail Privacy Protection. It routes emails through proxy servers and pre-loads all images, including tracking pixels, regardless of whether the user actually opened the email. This results in vastly inflated open rates.

### Gmail Image Caching
Google has been caching images on its own servers for years. While this protects users from malicious scripts, it also obscures accurate open data, as the sender's server only sees a hit from a Google proxy, not the actual recipient.

## A Reliable Alternative: Link-Based Tracking

If pixels are dead, how do you know if your email was successful? The answer lies in **Link Clicks**.

### High-Intent Engagement
An email "open" is a passive metric. The recipient might have accidentally clicked the email or skimmed it for two seconds. 

A link click, however, is an active sign of intent. When a recruiter clicks the link to your resume or portfolio, they are actively engaging with your profile.

### How Setu Does It
Platforms like **Setu** avoid invasive tracking pixels entirely. Instead, they use smart redirect links. When you send an email with Setu containing your resume link:
1. The link is securely wrapped.
2. When clicked, it briefly pings the server to register the "open".
3. The user is instantly redirected to your actual resume.

This method is immune to Apple MPP and Gmail caching because it requires an active user click.

## Why This Matters for Job Seekers

When you are sending cold outreach emails, you don't just want to know if an email was rendered on a screen—you want to know if the hiring manager actually looked at your resume.

By focusing on link clicks rather than pixel loads, you get accurate, actionable data. If a recruiter clicks your link 3 times, you know they are interested and it might be the perfect time to send a follow-up.

### Summary
Stop relying on outdated, inaccurate tracking pixels. Embrace link-based tracking for a privacy-friendly, highly accurate measure of true engagement.
