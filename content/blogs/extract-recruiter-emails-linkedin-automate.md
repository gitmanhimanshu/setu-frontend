## The Power of the Direct Email

When applying for jobs on platforms like LinkedIn or Naukri, the biggest challenge is visibility. Your resume is often ranked by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) or buried at the bottom of a pile of 500 candidates.

The most effective way to bypass this? Email the recruiter directly. 

But finding the email and writing the message takes time. Here is how you can use AI and Setu to automate the entire process.

### Step 1: Finding the Recruiter's Email
Often, recruiters will leave their email in the job description or their LinkedIn bio. If they don't, you can use LLMs to help deduce it.
- **Job Descriptions:** Simply paste the job description into your AI prompt. Ask the LLM, *"Extract any contact information, hiring manager names, or emails from this text."*
- **Company Format:** If you know the recruiter's name (e.g., Jane Doe at Acme Corp), the email format is usually `jane.doe@acmecorp.com` or `jdoe@acmecorp.com`.

### Step 2: The AI-Generated Draft
Once you have the email, you need a compelling message. Instead of writing it from scratch, use an LLM connected to Setu.

**Your Prompt:**
> "I want to email Jane Doe regarding the Senior Developer role at Acme Corp. Here is the job description and my resume. Draft a 100-word cold email."

The LLM will instantly generate a contextual draft that matches your skills to the specific role.

### Step 3: Sending with Setu
Instead of copying the draft, opening Gmail, pasting it, adding the subject line, and attaching your resume, you just tell Setu to execute it.

**Your Prompt:**
> "Looks good. Send it via Setu to jane.doe@acmecorp.com."

Setu will take the LLM's draft and securely send it through your Gmail account. It can even track if the recruiter clicks the links in your email!

### Takeaway
By combining the massive networks of LinkedIn and Naukri with the automation capabilities of Setu, you can reach out to 10x more recruiters per day, with 10x better personalization.
