## The Anatomy of a Perfect Cold Email

Writing a cold email that gets a response isn't about being overly clever; it's about being respectful, relevant, and concise. Before we dive into the templates, remember these three golden rules:
1. **Keep it under 150 words.** Busy people don't read essays.
2. **Focus on them, not you.** How can you solve *their* problem?
3. **Have a clear Call to Action (CTA).** Don't leave them guessing what to do next.

Here are five templates that follow these rules.

### 1. The "Direct Approach" (For Job Seekers)
**Subject:** Application for [Role] - [Your Name]

Hi [Name],

I noticed [Company] is hiring for a [Role]. I’ve been following your recent work on [Recent Project/News], and I’m incredibly impressed by the direction the team is taking.

I’m a [Your Profession] with [X] years of experience, recently specializing in [Specific Skill relevant to the job]. I’d love to bring my expertise in [Skill] to [Company] to help you achieve [Company Goal].

I’ve attached my resume for your review. Would you be open to a brief chat next week?

Best,  
[Your Name]

### 2. The "Value Add" (For Freelancers/Agencies)
**Subject:** A quick idea for [Company]'s [Specific Area]

Hi [Name],

I loved your recent article on [Topic]. It got me thinking about how [Company] handles [Specific Area].

I’ve helped companies like [Competitor/Similar Company] increase their [Metric] by [X]% by implementing [Your Solution]. I have a few quick ideas on how we could do the same for [Company].

Are you open to a 10-minute call this Thursday to discuss?

Best,  
[Your Name]

### 3. The "Networking/Informational Interview"
**Subject:** Inspired by your journey at [Company]

Hi [Name],

I’m currently exploring the [Industry] space and came across your profile. I’m fascinated by how you transitioned from [Past Role] to [Current Role] at [Company].

I know you’re incredibly busy, but would you have 10 minutes next week for a quick chat? I’d love to ask you a couple of questions about your experience. No expectations beyond that!

Thanks for your time,  
[Your Name]

### 4. The "Congratulatory" (Building Relationships)
**Subject:** Congrats on the [Recent Milestone]!

Hi [Name],

Just saw the news about [Company]'s recent [Milestone/Funding/Launch]. Huge congratulations to you and the team! 

I’m a big fan of what you’re building. I specialize in [Your Skill], and I’d love to stay in touch as you continue to grow. 

Keep up the great work!

Best,  
[Your Name]

### 5. The "Follow-Up"
**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

Hi [Name],

I know how easily emails get buried, so I just wanted to float this to the top of your inbox. 

If it’s not a good time right now, no worries at all. If you are open to a quick chat, I’m available [Day] or [Day].

Best,  
[Your Name]
