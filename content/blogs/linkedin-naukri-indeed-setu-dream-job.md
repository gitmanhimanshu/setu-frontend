## The Fragmented Job Search

If you are actively looking for a job, you know the struggle. You have 15 tabs open for LinkedIn, 10 for Naukri, and another 5 for Indeed. You find a great role, but the "Easy Apply" button feels like throwing your resume into a black hole.

To stand out, you need to email the recruiter or hiring manager directly. But doing this manually for every job—copying the description, finding the email, writing a tailored cover letter, and sending it—takes 30 minutes per application.

What if you could automate this entire workflow?

### The Setu Workflow

**Setu** bridges the gap between job boards and your inbox. By integrating large language models (LLMs) with Setu's automated email capabilities, you can turn a 30-minute chore into a 1-click process.

Here is how the ultimate job-seeking workflow operates:

#### 1. Sourcing (LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed)
You find a job posting on any major platform. Instead of reading it manually and trying to guess what the recruiter wants, you simply copy the job URL or text.

#### 2. Extraction (LLMs)
You pass the job details to an LLM (like Claude or ChatGPT) connected to Setu. The AI instantly reads the job description and extracts:
- The core skills required.
- The company's mission or current focus.
- The recruiter's email address (if listed).

#### 3. Personalization
The LLM compares these extracted requirements against your saved resume. It identifies exactly why you are a perfect fit and drafts a highly personalized, concise email that highlights your matching skills.

#### 4. Execution (Setu)
With a single prompt like *"Send my application for this role"*, Setu takes over. It securely accesses your Gmail via MCP, attaches your resume, and sends the drafted email directly to the recruiter. It also appends your professional Setu signature and tracking links.

### Why This Works

Recruiters are overwhelmed by generic applications submitted via portals. An email directly in their inbox stands out. But a *personalized* email that speaks directly to the pain points mentioned in their job description? That gets interviews.

### Try It Today

Stop letting the perfect job slip away because you didn't have time to write a custom cover letter. By combining the sourcing power of LinkedIn, Naukri, and Indeed with the execution power of Setu, you are no longer just participating in the job market—you are dominating it.
